Manifesto

We learn to perform, to improve, to move fast. Yet nobody teaches us to truly understand ourselves.

The result: young people adrift, who doubt, imitate, adapt, and end up disconnected from their inner compass.

MagSelf offers a safe space to explore, embrace, and share who we truly are. One purpose: to connect more deeply with ourselves, and with others.

What MagSelf does

Two levels of impact.

Inner

  • Understand your needs, your reactions, your patterns.
  • Identify what feels right and trust it.
  • Find what gives your life meaning.

Outer

  • Let your inner compass guide your choices.
  • Act with clarity, stability and confidence.
  • Feel grounded, validated and worthy.
Why it matters

The blind spot of our era: ignoring our inner nature.

Young people's well-being is suffering mainly from a lack of direction and self-knowledge. Living in the fog is exhausting.

Who is MagSelf for?

18–30 year-olds ready to discover themselves and flourish.

  • Those for whom doing things "right" is no longer enough.
  • Those who move forward, succeed… but find no meaning in it.
  • Those who live in the fog and struggle to make choices.
  • Those who are tired of shrinking themselves to fit.
  • Those who feel out of touch with their desires, their joy.
  • Those who want to stop running on autopilot.
  • Those who want to explore their inner world, but lack the tools or the time.

These signals are not weaknesses. They are invitations.

How does MagSelf work?

Four spaces. One ecosystem.

Exploration

A journey inward to understand your doubts, your choices and your challenges, with personalised guidance aligned with your inner nature, at every step.

Play

Grow through varied and personalised formats: games, videos, workshops. Self-discovery made enjoyable.

Orientation

Be guided towards the therapists best suited to your needs and your personality.

Connexion

Connect and grow with people who feel the same way, in anonymous, safe and caring spaces.
