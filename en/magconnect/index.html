Manifesto

We have learned to produce, consume, and discard — without questioning our relationship to materials or to one another.

The result: a disconnect from our natural impulse to cooperate, and resources left to fade away.

MagConnect recreates the conditions for cooperation between owners, experts, enthusiasts, and unused materials.

What MagConnect does

Two levels of impact.

MagConnect is more than a platform, it's a collective catalyst.

Human

  • Creating bonds.
  • Activating cooperation.
  • Revealing purpose, pride, and recognition.

Resource

  • Putting materials back in circulation.
  • Revealing their potential.
  • Passing on knowledge to repair, reuse, recycle, and repurpose.
Our first initiative: wood

Every region is full of materials, knowledge, and committed people. Yet these resources rarely cross paths.

MagConnect was born of a simple truth: one person's waste is another person's treasure.

Who is MagConnect for?
  • Those who love caring for wood and nature.
  • Those who love to teach as much as they love to learn.
  • Those who want to act differently.
  • Those who value what already exists.
  • Those who are looking for meaning, connection, and impact.
  • Those who want to grow individually through the collective.
How does MagConnect work?

Three spaces. One ecosystem.

Inspiring circle

Connecting wood enthusiasts to create, transform, and honour the material.

Wood marketplace

Circularity in action: give, sell, and find what can still be used.

Services marketplace
